Why this show exists

Chasing money will never get you there.

Once you become, money follows. Most people do it in reverse: they chase money and never become.

The trench mindset means chasing the journey instead of the destination. Seek first, and everything else gets added, like Matthew 6 says.

People in six-million-dollar homes get bored of them. But if they're busy, building and fulfilled, it's just the place they call home: they enjoy it like they enjoyed the small one.

The goal is to become so valuable that money stops mattering. Buy the big house, get bored, sell it, buy another. The car, the truck, the house: that stuff passes away. The man you became doesn't.
