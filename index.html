What I believe

You can do more than you can imagine.

I believe it because I'm living proof. The kid who grew up watching his mom work three jobs is now running companies, leading men, and building a family legacy with his wife and daughter.

I didn't get lucky. I didn't get a loan from my dad. I went down, over and over, and every time I came back up, I came back up bigger. That's the trench. You can't skip it. You can't pass it. You can only go through it.

This site is how I hand that to you: the story, the mindset, and the exact vehicles I use to turn guys who work hard into men who own something.

The Trench Curve

You have to go down before you go up.

This is my life on one line. Tap any point. Notice the lowest points set up the biggest climbs.

The Trench Curve · Bryan Wiggins · Field Record Every point is a win. Even the bottom.
Ground level The trench
  1. 01

    Grew up poor·Down · Still a win

    Single mother. Three jobs. Nothing handed to us.

    I watched my mom work three jobs to keep the lights on. That was my first lesson in what a provider actually does. No safety net, no shortcuts: just effort and a refusal to quit.

  2. 02

    Job Corps·Up · Win

    Job Corps: diploma, president, apprentice.

    Earned my high school diploma through Job Corps. Became campus and dorm president. Finished a carpentry apprenticeship and wildland firefighter training, and found a Green Beret mentor who flipped houses and ran businesses.

  3. 03

    Marine Corps·Up · Win

    Graduated on a Friday. Marine Corps on Monday.

    Iraq. Afghanistan. The 11th MEU. Combat action, honor graduate at corporals course, MCMAP black belt, and a combat instructor starting as a corporal. The Corps taught me standards, command and how to lead men under pressure.

  4. 04

    Failed businesses·Down · Still a win

    Solar. Expediting. An airport shuttle COVID killed.

    I built construction work on instructor off-cycles, then commercial solar, expediting and an executive-airport shuttle business COVID wiped out. Every failure was tuition. You have to go down before you go up.

  5. 05

    Serial entrepreneur·Up · Win

    Built it, sold it, built it bigger.

    Pressure washing in Nashville and Knoxville. Sold the Knoxville company. Studied social-media marketing with my wife in Brazil, then came home to build Charlie Company, Trench Made Society and The Social Hack.

  6. 06

    Nara & Evelyn·Up · Win

    A Brazilian wife and daughter who build beside me.

    I didn't build this and leave my family on the sidelines. Nara is in the trench with me: she runs the creative team at The Social Hack. Our daughter watches every rep. That's the scoreboard that matters.

  7. 07

    Inner circle·Up · Win

    The room changed the man.

    Today I sit with operators, investors and builders I used to only watch. The circle you keep decides the life you live. Now I build that room for the guys coming up behind me.

The Trench Mindset

Chase the becoming.Money follows.

Most men run it in reverse. They chase money and never become anything. The trench mindset is simple: chase the journey, not the destination, and everything else gets added.

Matthew 6:33

Exponential value

Stop chasing dollars. Start building something.

If you're chasing money, you're chasing the easy way: the better job offer, more hours, the next paycheck. And the biggest thing you're missing is exponential value.

The way most guys do it

Linear Value

You get paid when you work. You stop, the money stops.

  • Certified master mechanic. Valuable, while you're under the hood.
  • Top-tier truck driver. Valuable, while you're behind the wheel.
  • Chases the better offer. More hours, same ceiling.
  • Never becomes. Thirty years of reps, zero equity.
The trench way

Exponential Value

Build it right once. Reap it for the rest of your life.

  • A team of people who show up without you.
  • Operating procedures that run the day.
  • Systems and processes that don't need you on every job.
  • An asset with your name on it: that's how wealth is built.
