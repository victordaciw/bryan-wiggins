Bryan's story

No handouts.No shortcuts.100% in the trench.

Seven chapters. Two of them are the bottom. Every one of them is a win, because the bottom is where the man gets built.

Start at the bottom Skip to the offer

  1. 01

    The start

    Down · Still a win

    A single mother. Three jobs.

    I grew up poor. My mom worked three jobs to keep a roof over us, and I watched every hour of it.

    Nobody was coming to save us. There was no trust fund, no family business, no uncle with connections. That's where I learned the first rule of the trench: the provider doesn't get to quit. You find a way or you make one.

  2. 02

    Job Corps

    Up · Win

    The first time I chose the climb.

    I earned my high school diploma through Job Corps. I didn't just pass through: I became campus president and dorm president.

    I finished a carpentry apprenticeship and wildland firefighter training. And I found a mentor: a Green Beret who flipped houses and ran appliance businesses. He showed me a man could build things with his hands and his head.

  3. 03

    Marine Corps

    Up · Win

    Graduated Friday. Marine on Monday.

    I walked across the stage on a Friday and shipped to the Marine Corps the following Monday.

    Iraq. Afghanistan. The 11th MEU. Combat action. Honor graduate at corporals course. A MCMAP black belt. I became a combat instructor while I was still a corporal.

    The Corps gave me the operating system I still run today: standards, command intent, extreme ownership, and taking care of your men before yourself.

  4. 04

    The failures

    Down · Still a win

    I failed more than most men try.

    I built construction work on instructor off-cycles. When I got out, I started a commercial solar business. Then expediting. Then an executive-airport shuttle company, and COVID wiped it out.

    Every one of those was tuition. I learned how money really moves, how fast a business dies without systems, and how much a man can take and still get up. You have to go down before you go up.

  5. 05

    Serial entrepreneur

    Up · Win

    Built it. Sold it. Built it bigger.

    I started pressure washing in Nashville and Knoxville and built it into a real company. Then I sold the Knoxville business.

    My wife and I went to Brazil and studied social-media marketing together. We came home and built Charlie Company with my war brother Stephen Daniels, then Trench Made Society, The Social Hack and the In The Trench Podcast on top of it.

  6. 06

    Family

    Up · Win

    A Brazilian wife and a daughter who build with me.

    Nara isn't standing behind me. She's next to me in the trench: she runs the creative team at The Social Hack. Our daughter has seen the wins and the losses up close.

    That's the part most guys miss. Building a business and leaving your family on the sidelines isn't winning. Building it together is.

  7. 07

    The inner circle

    Up · Win

    The room changed the man.

    Today I sit at tables with operators, investors and builders I used to only watch on a screen.

    Here's what I learned: the circle you keep decides the life you live. So now I build that room for the next guy, and I hand him the keys.

Why I tell it

I can't believe the life I'm living.You can build yours.
