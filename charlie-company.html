Owner #001 · Stephen Daniels

He sold everything in Wisconsin.

Stephen is my war brother. Certified master mechanic. Top-tier professional truck driver. Good at everything he touched.

He thought he was already at the bottom. He wasn't. He was living a comfortable life: no risk, no reward, and the results he had were a reflection of that. I showed him that.

So he sold it all, left Wisconsin, and invested heavily into owning Charlie Company Nashville. He chose to go down so he could finally go up.

The math nobody teaches you

A great tradesman is only valuable while he's working.

Stephen was building himself into the best mechanic and the best driver he could be. Respectable. But the second he stops, the value stops. Build out a location instead: learn to run a business and lead a team, and you build something that pays you whether or not you're the one holding the wrench.

The way most guys do it

Linear Value

You get paid when you work. You stop, the money stops.

  • Certified master mechanic. Valuable, while you're under the hood.
  • Top-tier truck driver. Valuable, while you're behind the wheel.
  • Chases the better offer. More hours, same ceiling.
  • Never becomes. Thirty years of reps, zero equity.
The trench way

Exponential Value

Build it right once. Reap it for the rest of your life.

  • A team of people who show up without you.
  • Operating procedures that run the day.
  • Systems and processes that don't need you on every job.
  • An asset with your name on it: that's how wealth is built.

What it looks like

You buy in. We build it with you.

This is not a franchise where you get a binder and a logo and good luck. My team and I stand the location up next to you.

  1. You buy in

    You own a percentage of your Charlie Company location. Real ownership, under a formal agreement.

  2. We start the location up

    Bryan and the team launch your market with you, on the ground, not on a Zoom call.

  3. Front and back office installed

    CRM, scheduling, estimating, sales process, invoicing, bookkeeping workflow and the KPI board: implemented, not handed over in a PDF.

  4. Every account set up

    Software, vendors, suppliers and marketing accounts are configured for you so day one is about jobs, not logins.

  5. The proven brand, plugged in

    You launch under a brand that already has an audience, a reputation and a content engine behind it.

  6. Trained on every aspect

    How to hire workers. How to get jobs. How to deliver them. How to collect. How to lead the team. Basically everything about the business.

  7. One year inside Trench Made Society

    The full library, the videos and real-time coaching from Bryan and the room, for a full year.

Here's what's crazy about it

The coaching alone is worth more than the buy-in.

Top-tier business coaches charge six and seven figures for the kind of access you get as a partner. Here, it's part of your deal.

You're not buying a job. You're buying a business, a team that builds it with you, and a year in the room that teaches you to run it.
